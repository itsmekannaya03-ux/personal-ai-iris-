## Member Mapping
When someone says "I'm M4", read the STATUS.md in their folder.

| Says | Name | Read This |
|------|------|-----------|
| "I'm M1" | Full-Stack Lead | `website/STATUS.md` + `api/` |
| "I'm M2" | AI Engine Dev | `app/STATUS.md` |
| "I'm M4" | UI/UX Designer | `designs/STATUS.md` |
| "I'm M8" | Content Manager | `docs/content/STATUS.md` |
| "I'm M3/M5/M6/M7/M9/M10" | Non-coding | Their README.md |

## How to Respond
1. Read their STATUS.md
2. Read the key files in their folder
3. Tell them what's done, what's pending, and what to do next
