# Long-Term Vision

## Purpose
Define the ultimate long-term vision for IRIS — what it aspires to become beyond any current roadmap or technical limitation.

## Responsibilities
- Articulate the 10-year vision for IRIS
- Inspire sustained effort and commitment
- Guide strategic decision-making
- Define success in the long term

## Design Goals
- **Ambitious**: Vision that feels impossible but compelling
- **Grounded**: Connected to current work and values
- **Inspiring**: Makes people want to contribute
- **Enduring**: Relevant for 10+ years

## Components

### The Ultimate Goal
IRIS becomes the world's first true Personal AI Operating System — an invisible, omnipresent intelligence that enhances human capability without compromising human autonomy or privacy.

### The 10-Year Vision

#### 2026-2027: Foundation
- IRIS is a capable local-first AI assistant
- Strong privacy guarantees
- Active open-source community

#### 2027-2028: Intelligence
- IRIS M1/M2 models outperform general models on personal tasks
- Adaptive intelligence that learns from each user
- Proactive assistance that anticipates needs

#### 2028-2029: Ubiquity
- IRIS runs on all devices (desktop, mobile, wearable)
- Cross-device mesh with end-to-end encryption
- Voice-first interaction is primary interface

#### 2029-2030: Autonomy
- IRIS handles complex multi-step tasks autonomously
- Users delegate decisions to IRIS with trust
- IRIS negotiates on behalf of users

#### 2030-2031: Symbiosis
- IRIS augments human intelligence in real-time
- Seamless human-AI collaboration
- AI that makes humans smarter, not dependent

#### 2032+: Transcendence
- IRIS Foundation Model powers everything
- AI that respects human values autonomously
- Open platform for third-party AI innovation
- Privacy as the default, not a feature

### Core Principles That Never Change
1. **Privacy Always**: Users own their data and their AI
2. **Local by Default**: Processing on device unless explicitly authorized
3. **User Sovereignty**: Users control their AI, not the reverse
4. **Open Platform**: Anyone can extend, modify, and improve IRIS
5. **Human Augmentation**: IRIS makes humans smarter, not replaced

## Data Flow
The vision influences every strategic decision: architecture, features, partnerships, and business model.

## Public Interfaces
The vision is communicated publicly through the manifesto, website, and community engagement.

## Configuration
The vision is immutable in principle but may evolve in expression over time.

## Dependencies
The vision depends on:
- Advances in AI research
- Privacy-preserving technology
- Community adoption and contribution
- Sustainable development model (open source + commercial)

## Security Considerations
The long-term vision maintains privacy as a fundamental right, not a feature tradeoff.

## Failure Recovery
If the vision proves impossible, adapt the vision — but never compromise on privacy.

## Future Improvements
The long-term vision defines what future improvements should aim for.

## Engineering Notes
Every line of code should move toward this vision. If a feature doesn't, question whether it belongs in IRIS.

## Acceptance Criteria
- The vision is documented and communicated
- All major decisions reference the vision
- The vision remains consistent with the manifesto
- The vision inspires contributions
- The vision is revisited and reaffirmed annually
